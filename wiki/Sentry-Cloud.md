# Sentry Cloud

**Your drive and charging history, in your browser. Free, and encrypted so only you can read it.**

Sentry Cloud is the free companion service to Sentry USB, run by Scott and Jhoan. Your Pi uploads its drive and charging history over Wi-Fi, and you can review it from any computer or phone by signing in at **[sentryusb.com](https://sentryusb.com)**.

## What you get

- **Drive history**: every synced drive with its route, distance, duration and FSD usage, plus route playback.
- **Charging history**: charging sessions with curves, energy used and costs.
- **A map of all your drives** in one place.
- **Sharing**: share one drive as a link, choosing which part of the route and which stats it shows.
- **3 GB of cloud storage** with every account. Sentry Cloud is free, with no paid plans.

## What it stores

- Drive and charging history from your paired Pi.
- **Dashcam video stays on your Pi** and your own archive. Sentry Cloud stores drive data, not video.
- Your history is **encrypted on the Pi before it leaves your network**, and it's unlocked in your browser when you sign in.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> When you create your account, save your **recovery key** somewhere safe, such as a password manager. If you lose every way to unlock your history and the recovery key, support can't recover it.

## Who can sign up

You need to be **18 or older** and live in the **US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Hong Kong, Singapore or Japan**. You can still sign in while traveling.

## Get started

1. Create a free account at **[sentryusb.com](https://sentryusb.com)** and save your recovery key.
2. On sentryusb.com, open **Settings → Devices** and get a pairing code.
3. On your Pi, open `http://sentryusb.local`, go to **Settings → Car & Network**, find the **SentryCloud** card, enter the code and click **Pair**.

Your Pi then uploads your history whenever it's on Wi-Fi. If you reflash or replace your Pi, pair it again; your existing history stays in your account.

You can also sign in from the Sentry Connect app on iPhone and iPad.
